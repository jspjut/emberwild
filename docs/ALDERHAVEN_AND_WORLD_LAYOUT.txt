EMBERWILD · ORGANIC REACH AND ALDERHAVEN
5 November 2026 · layout revision 3

Playable geography

The northern Hearthstead route retains its authored first-hour landmarks, pool,
memorials and quest state. The six outer camps no longer occupy matching columns
and rows. Wayfarer's Watch occupies an eastern shoulder with a separate capital
approach. Tideglass is northeast above Saltglass Mere. Rootsong sits in a western
valley; Stonepromise climbs toward its southwest vault. Ashen Hearth lies in a
southern hollow and Reedwatch follows southeastern pools and a different bank.
Roads bend through intermediate clearings rather than connecting equivalent grid
cells. Existing cave/storehouse plans remain enterable and distinct from the camp.
The one-to-ten campaign, quest IDs and reward budget are unchanged.

The Reach keeps its prior area envelope, approximately 334,080 square metres.
Alderhaven occupies an approximately 840 by 780 metre city envelope, with its wall
inside that boundary. That is a capital-scale prototype footprint, not a claim of
measured equality to any particular commercial game's map. Continuous shared
coordinates make the eastern road walkable without a scene load or teleport.
Collision still restricts departure elsewhere; remaining Reach exits stay reserved.
Player map IDs update on the server when crossing, and saved position/map state,
chat zone membership, return travel, atlas projection and Find me use that geography.
The 64-player cap is shared by this server; the city is not a separate 64-player shard.

Alderhaven

Eight wards organize services, a civic skyline, narrower homes and garden paths:
  Open Gate: western arrival arch, Unlatched Door inn and relief house.
  Fourwinds Exchange: Common Vault bank, provisioner and market hearth.
  Concord Heights: large Hall of the Open Hand and the First Speaker.
  Unfinished Archive: copy desks, shelves and Keeper Ysra Vey.
  Bellfounders' Close: the House of Bells and its unstruck memorial bell.
  Lantern Gardens: five open guest pavilions and Lantern House inn.
  Lowdoor Ward: common house and a neighborhood with its own representative.
  Willow Quays: rescue hall and the eastern river.

All 45 city buildings have accessible ground floors through shared door collision.
The Assembly, Archive and foundry have larger silhouettes, higher interiors and
rooftop towers. Civic portico columns collide. Windows, bands, banners and cutaway
roofs distinguish large halls from scaled-up starter cottages. Roof towers are
part of the same cutaway group as their building. Decorative window/roof detail
is not a separate climbable interior or a promised upper floor.

City landmarks use original geometric art. Interiors currently remain prototype
rooms and furniture, not finished bespoke multi-floor architecture. City streets
have ample camera room; no mount, transit, auction house, guild, housing or capital
quest system is introduced by the city layout.

The NPC principals are First Speaker Aveline Rook, Keeper Ysra Vey, Master Corven
Bell, Tessa Wren and Maelin of the Open Hand. Their appearances use five distinct
NPC-only ceremonial sets, individualized hair/builds and highlighted nameplates.
Dialogue offers authored topic choices; one Speaker topic requires completed
five_roads. The server checks topic ownership, quest state, range, sight, life and
request rate. Spoken gestures accompany the private reading/dialogue text. No XP,
items, reputation, player ability or repeated material reward is granted.

Maraen Ledger opens the existing 28-slot personal bank at the Common Vault.
Right-click in bags deposits one; right-click in the bank withdraws one; Shift
transfers a stack. Cursor and tooltip behavior reuse the established bank system.
Three innkeepers offer explicit voluntary binds: gate, market and garden hearths.
The eight-second return and fifteen-minute cooldown retain their prior semantics
and save/offline handling. Merely speaking to an innkeeper does not rebind a player.

Story continuity

The author file game-data/story/alderhaven.json expands civic history and records
which open threads have advanced. Maelin is Sera's living sister. Tessa's father
escaped through Anja's low passage; Lowdoor has a continuing ordinary life. Ysra is
Annet's granddaughter. The correction letter exists, but its contested copies and
the intent behind batch seven are still unresolved. Optional city conversations
must not revoke the Reach's successful local conclusion or assign a quest that
does not exist. The city has no quest population yet.

Atlas and expansion

shared/world-plan.js allocates 35 content slots: 20 leveling zones across levels
1–100, five capitals (one per playable race) and ten level-100 frontiers with two
inspirations per race. Inspiration does not impose ownership or racial access.
Only cinderwood and alderhaven are assigned. Future entries have no fixed name,
position, playable promise or artificial quest count. Add their regions when their
story and geography justify them; do not show the slot ledger as an in-game menu.

The known world retains drawn parchment and untouched survey edges. The regional
chart now opens either the Reach or the city. The city chart sketches streets,
roof blocks, ward washes, walls and the river. Projection derives the correct map
from authored mapId or shared coordinates, so a Reach quest does not become a city
objective merely because the player is in Alderhaven. Other-player markers use
their own map. The minimap's cached background spans both areas and includes city
roads, buildings and river. Right-click moves up the atlas only, as before.

For a future zone:
  Choose one reserved content slot and record its map ID and actual progression.
  Add an appropriate region/child entry to ATLAS_MAPS, with independent authored
  parchment placement, survey outline and world bounds.
  Confirm hit/navigation, ancestor projection and unique slot allocation.
  Build shared geographic/movement boundaries before saying an exit is open.
  Revisit the progression budget and story thread registry.
  Add real path/door/interaction checks and update affected save migration.

Signs and rendering

Road boards now use one extruded arrow polygon. The triangular head shares the
outline with its rectangular stem, replacing the rotated three-sided cone that
left gaps. Each board retains its destination association; post hover stays empty.
Signs point to camps, shelter routes, remaining exits and city services/wards.
Their post collision remains shared with prediction and authority.

Terrain now keeps a bounded window of 361 tiles around the player, with the same
fine/coarse surface logic and disposal when moving away. This permits a larger
world without allocating a mesh for every future region. Road segments are bucketed
for local terrain/foliage searches. Building visibility extends far enough in the
city for its skyline; northern fog and existing local camera behavior are retained.
These changes do not establish production-scale world streaming or server sharding.

Migration and verification

Revision-two positions follow the nearest moved camp or shelter anchor, while the
northern starter landmarks and town/farm anchors stay fixed. Legacy revision-one
positions still migrate to the new placements. Invalid outlying legacy coordinates
are clamped before normal surface validation. Character identity, quest state,
items, bags, bank, reputation and return cooldowns remain intact. A valid migrated
save records revision 3 so the transform does not repeat.

collision, progression, banking, city topics, city hearths and migration.
npm run test:capital uses a temporary server and five disposable characters. It
walks over the actual boundary, checks city/regional/Reach atlas views, enters the

City life and raised terrain (5 November 2026)

Two wardens now patrol the arrival road and civic approach. Pip and Miri walk from
Lowdoor to a schoolhouse and back, with lesson/home stops on a short game-time
circuit rather than a real-day timetable. Bessa Basket walks a market circuit,
stops at bends and offers the normal provision stock. Gestures and conversations
pause these actors; a merchant transaction checks her current authoritative
position, not her authored spawn. The minimap/atlas service marker follows a
subscribed traveling merchant. NPC gait and body/name anchors interpolate locally.

The city now has a high civic plateau, an archive rise, garden banks and lower
quays. Buildings retain level floor pads at their local elevation; streets and
approaches use smooth grades. The market hearth footprint and one pavilion were
adjusted to remove pre-existing building overlaps exposed by the different floor
heights. Schoolhouse is a renamed existing home, not another quest hub.

The arrival road bends through a wider approach. Road width, ground palette and
fog blend gradually, with trees, a cart and a wayside light tying the two areas
together. The redundant border portal posts/lamp were removed; the actual city
arch is the entrance. The ownership handoff occurs within this approach and is
separate from the physical gate. See ZONE_REPLICATION.txt for the live update and
simulation boundaries. Older characters settle onto the new ground while keeping
identity, inventory, quest progress and bank data; no character wipe is needed.
